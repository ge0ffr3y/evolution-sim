Evolution Sim

Simple organisms evolve in a changing world. Nobody is told to be a predator, a plant eater or a scavenger: those roles, arms races, new species and colonies of cells emerge from random mutation and one rule, everything costs energy.

Watch a world

Each opens the simulator with a world that shows something well. Runs are seeded, so the same link always plays out the same way.

How to watch

  • Click an organism to inspect it: its genes, energy, family, and (in worlds with brains) its brain thinking.
  • Color the dots by diet, size, palate and more, and switch the map between plants, temperature, terrain and other overlays.
  • Scroll down for the population, diet and trait charts and the family tree of species.
  • Copy link shares the exact run you are watching.
