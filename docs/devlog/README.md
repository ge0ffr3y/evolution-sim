# Devlog

One entry per phase or part of a phase, from an empty repository to living plants: what was built, what emerged, what broke, and one image each. Newest last. For the results in one place, see [What we found](../findings/README.md).

## Building the world (Phases 0 to 3, v0.1)

0. **[Foundations](0000-foundations.md)**. Phase 0. No life yet, but the ground rules are in place.
1. **[A minimal living world](0001-minimal-living-world.md)**. Phase 1. The first dots, and the first evolution.
2. **[Genes and trade-offs](0002-genes-and-tradeoffs.md)**. Phase 2. Every trait now has a price, and two different ways of life share one world.
3. **[Food webs](0003-food-webs.md)**. Phase 3, and the v0.1 release. Nobody is told to hunt, and in every seed something learns to.

## Brains and species (Phases 4 and 5, v0.2)

4. **[Senses and brains](0004-senses-and-brains.md)**. Phase 4. Prey learned to run.
5. **[Species and family trees](0005-species-and-family-trees.md)**. Phase 5. Life now has a history you can read.

## A changing world and ecology (Phases 6 and 7, v0.3)

6. **[A dynamic world](0006-a-dynamic-world.md)**. Phase 6. The world stops being a lawn.
7. **[Ecological depth](0007-ecological-depth.md)**. Phase 7. Bodies wear out, parents choose between many cheap babies and few fed ones, pathogens hunt the common genotype, colors become disguises and signals, and kin recognition fixes one problem by creating a bigger one.

## Multicellularity and the grand world (Phase 8, v0.4)

8. **[From cells to bodies](0008-multicellularity.md)**. Phase 8. Cells can stay together, share food, specialize and bond with strangers.
9. **[Closing open questions](0009-open-questions.md)**. A short cleanup between Phase 8 and Phase 9. Four loose ends from earlier phases, checked with data instead of left as "not measured".
10. **[The grand world](0010-grand-world.md)**. Phase 8b. Evolvable social genes, richer environments, kinds of plant, memory and chemical signals for brains, and colonies with brains, all switchable and all available together in one large world.
11. **[Making the new features pay](0011-making-features-pay.md)**. Phase 8c. After devlog 10 found that several new features did not evolve on their own, I went back through each one and asked whether it could pay in principle.
12. **[Sex in the grand world](0012-sex-in-the-grand-world.md)**. Phase 8d. Sex spread in simple worlds (devlog 11) but faded in the grand world.
13. **[Ecology overhaul](0013-ecology-overhaul.md)**. Phase 8e, first half. The 5-million-tick run showed a world that was stable but dull: food gone bare in patches, nothing ever left a scar, nothing ever struck at random.
14. **[Nutrients, climate biomes and colonies](0014-opus-batch.md)**. Phase 8e, second half. Four bigger changes: nutrients that travel through food, climate biomes with water and ice, colony physics with shared senses, and a long hunt for why meat eating fails in the grand world.
15. **[Learning brains, and where memory pays](0015-learning-brains.md)**. Phase 8f. Until now, a brain's wiring was fixed at birth: only evolution, across generations, could change it.

## Experiments at scale (Phase 9)

16. **[Auditing the grand world](0016-grand-world-audit.md)**. Phase 9, part 1. Before running long experiments, every factor in the grand world was put to the test: each mechanism switched off, each of about 300 settings halved and doubled, each unused feature switched on, and every finding confirmed on fresh seeds before anything changed.
17. **[Headline experiments](0017-headline-experiments.md)**. Phase 9, part 2. With the grand world audited, five questions it can now answer, each on 16 fresh seeds with a chart and a written conclusion.
18. **[The missing meat eaters](0018-the-missing-meat-eaters.md)**. Phase 9, part 3. No headline experiment produced a lasting group of meat eaters, so this was a hunt for the reason: a diagnosis of who earns what, three new settings, a screen of seven changes, and two "invasion" tests that drop ready-made meat eaters into the grand world.
19. **[Where the meat eaters went](0019-where-the-meat-eaters-went.md)**. Phase 9, part 4. Devlog 18 left lasting meat eaters as an open question.
20. **[Margins, and fat that pays](0020-margins-and-fat.md)**. Phase 9, part 5. The goal was meat eaters in the grand world itself, not only in its own "hunters" version.
21. **[A richer grand world](0021-a-richer-grand-world.md)**. Phase 9, part 6. A short one.
22. **[Mating and sight](0022-mating-and-sight.md)**. Phase 9, part 7. The owner's ideas: attraction (choosing mates by diet), a scent that helps ready mates find each other, and an eye that could specialize: narrow and sharp for hunters, wide for prey.
23. **[Predator life](0023-predator-life.md)**. Phase 9, part 8. The owner's ideas: real predators digest slowly, rest a lot and take the easiest prey.
24. **[Living plants](0024-living-plants.md)**. Phase 9, part 9. The owner's idea: plants were too simple.
