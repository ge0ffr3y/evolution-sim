# Evolution Sim v1.0.1

A fix for narrow windows.

**Live:** https://ge0ffr3y.github.io/evolution-sim/

## Fixed

- **The simulator's side panel no longer runs past the edge of a narrow window.** Its drop-down menus size themselves to their longest option, and the World menu's longest preset name made everything in the panel 414 pixels wide inside a 320-pixel panel. The panel and its menus now shrink to fit, and long readouts wrap.
- **The "Map shows" menu** under the world (whose longest option is very long) no longer makes the page wider than a tablet or phone screen.
- **The charts follow the window when it gets narrower.** Before, they kept the width they were first drawn at, so the page scrolled sideways after the window was narrowed. They now check their size every time they redraw.

Checked at 1,280, 1,000, 900, 821, 820 and 600 pixels wide and at phone width: nothing sticks out of the side panel, and the page never scrolls sideways.
