# Evolution Sim v1.0

A browser-based artificial-life simulation where predators, plant eaters, scavengers, species and colonies emerge from mutation and one rule: everything costs energy.

**Live:** https://ge0ffr3y.github.io/evolution-sim/

## New since v0.4

- **A front page** with six featured worlds, each starting in one click: the grand world (now the default), food webs from nothing, a predator arms race, an ice age, two continents, and living plants.
- **Shareable links.**
  - A Copy link button.
  - Links carry the seed, the world and any changed settings, so anyone can replay the exact run.
  - Old links still work.
- **What we found:** a visitor's summary of the results, honest about what did not work.
- **The devlog as a series:** 25 entries grouped by phase, all readable on the site.
- **Experiments at scale (Phase 9).**
  - A cloud runner: one on-demand AWS machine per experiment, a cost ceiling the owner approves, and resumable runs.
  - Paired comparisons with 95% ranges and red flags.
  - An experiment results page.
  - An audit of the grand world (about 3,200 worlds).
  - Five headline experiments: sex, ice ages, disease, barriers, plant quality.
- **The meat-eater hunt.** Why the full grand world keeps no lasting meat eaters, and what was tried:
  - sparing family;
  - fat that pays;
  - mating by diet;
  - a specializing eye;
  - a slow gut;
  - prey condition;
  - injury;
  - young that move away;
  - a kill autopsy.

  A "Grand world: hunters" preset shows the world that does grow them.
- **Living plants** (all optional): plants that evolve to fit their land, poisonous plants, and dietary nutrients.
- **Testing on GitHub:** every push runs the type check, the full test suite and the build.

Every new feature is off by default, and worlds saved with earlier versions still load.

## Known open problems

- No lasting meat-eating group in the full grand world (see What we found).
- Brains learn to flee and ambush, but not to chase.
- In a narrow window the simulator's side panel runs past the edge of the screen (to be fixed in the user-interface work after v1.0).
